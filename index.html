Know before you donate

Good information saves time

Learn the basics, understand the process, and always follow the screening advice of an authorised blood bank.

🩸

Am I eligible?

In India, whole-blood donors are generally required to be 18–65 years old and meet minimum weight and haemoglobin criteria. A clinician makes the final decision after screening.

Official health information ↗
🩺

What happens?

Registration, a confidential health questionnaire, basic checks, donation by trained staff, and a short rest with refreshments. Follow the blood bank's after-care advice.

Find a partner centre ↗
💡

Myth or fact?

Healthy eligible people can usually donate safely. The body replaces the donated fluid, and red blood cells are replenished over time. Screening keeps donors and recipients safer.
